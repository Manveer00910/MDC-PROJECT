About the project

HydraBytes

The proposed system explores how multi-source environmental data could support localized flash-flood risk assessment in hilly regions. It is designed as a decision-support concept for communities and disaster-management teams.

INPUT LAYER
Environmental data

Rainfall, weather, satellite observations, stream levels, terrain and elevation.

PROCESSING
Data + ML

Cleaning, feature extraction and a future trained risk-classification model.

OUTPUT
Risk dashboard

Risk category, GIS-style visualization and localized warning interface.

PROPOSED STACK
Python + GIS

Python, Pandas, NumPy, scikit-learn, mapping, weather APIs and a cloud database.

Proposed workflow

01
Data collection & cleaningGather and validate weather, rainfall, water-level and terrain information.
02
Feature extractionConvert raw measurements into useful indicators for risk assessment.
03
Risk predictionTrain and validate a model when suitable labelled historical data is available.
04
GIS dashboard & alertsPresent localized risk levels and connect to verified alert channels in a later phase.

Research directions listed in the project brief include IMD, ISRO/Earth Observation resources, Central Water Commission, DEM/GIS datasets, and historical rainfall and flash-flood records.
